Americas

US revokes visas of 27 Latin American officials over corruption, drug-trafficking ties

The State Department said Monday it is revoking or denying visas to 27 current and former officials from Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador and Peru, including Bolivian Attorney General Roger Mariaca, over accusations of bribery and aiding narcotrafficking. The department said the action fulfills "commitments to defend democratic governance" in the hemisphere, weeks after Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited the three Andean nations to pledge support for their anti-trafficking efforts.

Sources: WRAL (AP), US State Department

Europe

Russian missile and drone barrage hits Kyiv, striking National Academy of Sciences

Russia launched a combined ballistic-missile and drone attack on Kyiv early Tuesday, striking the National Academy of Sciences and other civilian sites and setting off explosions across the capital as air-defense systems activated during an hours-long alert. The previous day's strikes on the city had already killed two people and injured 26, Kyiv authorities said, and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said "science became a target for Russia" in condemning the attacks. A final casualty count for Tuesday's strikes was not yet confirmed.

Sources: US News (Reuters), Kyiv Post

Poland scrambles jets, briefly shuts two airports after Russian drone strikes near border

Poland closed the Lublin and Rzeszów airports and scrambled military aircraft overnight Sunday into Monday after Russian forces struck an area near Ukraine's border with a jet-powered drone, the latest in a string of airspace incidents along NATO's eastern flank in recent weeks. Flight operations resumed within hours once the threat had passed, and Polish officials reported no casualties.

Sources: Liga.net (Reuters)

Middle East

Over 100 Israeli settlers attack West Bank village, injuring security forces

More than 100 Israeli settlers rioted in the village of Jalud, near Nablus, early Tuesday, burning two Palestinian homes and injuring three Israeli security personnel who were escorting a displaced family's court-ordered return. The attack drew unusually sharp criticism from Israeli officials, including Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who blamed "a small group of rioters," while police said they arrested one adult and detained two minors for questioning.

Sources: France 24 (AFP), Al Jazeera

Africa

Ethiopia accuses Eritrea, Sudan and Egypt of backing Tigray rebels as they seize Afar town

Ethiopia's army chief, Field Marshal Birhanu Jula, said Sunday that Eritrea is financing Tigrayan rebel forces and suggested Sudan and Egypt are also aiding them, as fighting escalated following the rebel coalition's weekend capture of the strategic Afar town of Erebti. Eritrea, Sudan and Egypt denied involvement, and the widening accusations have fueled fears that Ethiopia's renewed internal conflict could spill into a broader regional war.

Sources: US News (AP), Al Jazeera

Asia

China's Wang Yi tells Japan ties must be "normalized" in first high-level contact since Taiwan rift

Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi met former Japanese foreign minister Takeshi Iwaya in Beijing on Monday, the first official meeting between a Japanese politician and a top Chinese official since bilateral relations soured last November over Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's remarks on a Taiwan contingency. Wang told Iwaya, who led a business delegation seeking to ease Chinese trade restrictions, that ties face "a severe situation" and urged Tokyo to correct its leaders' stance on Taiwan.

Sources: Japan Today (Kyodo News), Nikkei Asia

Thailand's flood death toll climbs to 23 as Bangkok disaster zone remains underwater

Thailand's disaster agency said Tuesday the nationwide death toll from weeks of monsoon flooding had risen to 23 after four more deaths in Bangkok, with roughly 700,000 people affected across the capital and floodwaters covering parts of 29 of the country's 77 provinces. In neighboring Myanmar, landslides and flooding in the southern Tanintharyi region have killed at least eight people, with local officials saying 20 to 30 more remain missing.

Sources: IOL (AFP), Manila Times
