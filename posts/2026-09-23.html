Americas

US and 14 Latin American governments sign "Shield of the Americas" pact, target 24 criminal groups

On the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, the United States and 14 Latin American and Caribbean governments signed a joint declaration Tuesday formalizing the "Shield of the Americas" security coalition, pledging asset freezes, visa bans and criminal prosecution for members of 24 designated criminal organizations, including Mexican cartels, Venezuela's Tren de Aragua and Brazil's Comando Vermelho and Primeiro Comando da Capital. The coalition, pushed by President Trump since March, now covers nearly the entire hemisphere outside Mexico, Brazil, Cuba and Canada.

Sources: Gulf News, Al Jazeera

Milei tells UN Argentina won't back down in Falklands oil dispute as Britain vows to "stand firm"

Argentine President Javier Milei addressed the UN General Assembly Tuesday amid rising tension with Britain over an offshore oil exploration project near the Falkland Islands, after Argentina opened sanctions proceedings against more than 60 companies and individuals it says are linked to the drilling. British Prime Minister Andy Burnham told the same assembly that Britain would "stand firm" over the islands, reviving a sovereignty dispute Argentina says violates a 1976 UN resolution urging both sides toward dialogue.

Sources: US News (Reuters), Mining Weekly (Reuters)

Europe

EU extends Russia sanctions three more years after agreeing to drop two billionaires from the list

The Council of the European Union voted Tuesday to extend individual sanctions tied to Russia's war on Ukraine until 2029, resolving a days-long standoff by removing billionaires Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman from the list after France and Luxembourg pushed for their delisting. Ukraine's foreign minister Andrii Sybiha called the trade-off "shameful and unjustifiable," while Latvia, the last holdout against the deal, ultimately abstained rather than block the broader renewal.

Sources: Yahoo (AP), Brussels Times

Zelensky says Ukraine ready for energy truce with Russia after meeting Trump at UN

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky met President Trump on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly Tuesday and said afterward that Ukraine is ready for a full ceasefire on energy infrastructure, including oil and gas refineries, if Russia commits to the same terms. Zelensky said the two also discussed winter energy support and licenses for Ukraine to produce Patriot missile interceptors, as Kyiv weighs halting a refinery-strike campaign it views as its strongest leverage over Moscow.

Sources: Yahoo (AP), Al Jazeera

Middle East

Houthi rebels' fast advance down Yemen's Red Sea coast has killed over 670, displaced more than 150,000

Iran-backed Houthi rebels seized Yemen's Red Sea coast, including the port of Mocha, from pro-government forces in less than 48 hours earlier this month and are now fighting to take the Kahboub Mountains in their biggest land grab in years. Renewed fighting has killed at least 674 people and displaced more than 158,000, reigniting Yemen's 12-year civil war and threatening both Saudi Arabia's oil exports and the wider Red Sea shipping route.

Sources: Yahoo (AP), Al Jazeera

Iran says it can reopen Strait of Hormuz within a week, hints at reviving talks with US

A senior Iranian official told Reuters Tuesday that Iran could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if Washington eases military pressure and lifts its blockade of Iranian ports, adding that Iran's UN delegation had been authorized by the supreme leader to revive diplomacy with the United States. No direct meeting between President Trump and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian was scheduled, but the two countries' delegations held hours of indirect talks through mediators on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.

Sources: US News (Reuters), Yahoo (Reuters)

Africa

Nigerians protest as 600 remain captive a month after mass kidnapping; some hostages reported executed

Residents of Nigeria's Borgu district protested Monday over the fate of roughly 600 to 700 people, mostly women, children and the elderly, still missing a month after gunmen attacked several communities near the border with Benin in one of the country's largest mass kidnappings. President Bola Tinubu ordered a rescue operation on Aug. 25, but the captives have not been found, and reports have emerged that several hostages have been executed, adding pressure on his government ahead of January 2027 elections.

Sources: US News (AP), Yahoo (AP)

UN voices "deep concern" as seven Ethiopian armed groups, including old civil-war foes, ally to oust Abiy Ahmed

The United Nations said Tuesday it was "deeply concerned" by Ethiopia's worsening security situation, a day after seven armed and political groups, including the Tigray People's Liberation Front and the Amhara Fano movement, announced a coalition aimed at overthrowing Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's government. The groups fought on opposite sides of the 2020-2022 Tigray war, and a UN spokesperson pointed to the new alliance, continued instability and reports of drone strikes as fueling regional risk.

Sources: US News (AP), allAfrica.com

Asia

Pakistan says airstrikes killed 28 militants in Afghanistan; Kabul reports three civilians dead

Pakistani fighter jets struck suspected militant hideouts in Afghanistan's eastern Kunar province Monday, with Pakistani security officials claiming 28 fighters were killed, while Afghan authorities said the strikes killed three civilians, including a woman and a girl. The UN mission in Afghanistan confirmed at least three civilian deaths and four injuries, and the strikes came after Pakistan warned it would hit militants inside Afghan territory following two deadly attacks on its own border forces.

Sources: US News (AP), Washington Post (AP)

Xi Jinping arrives in Washington for first state visit since 2015 as Trump summit opens

Chinese President Xi Jinping and his wife Peng Liyuan were due to arrive at Andrews Air Force Base Wednesday for a three-day state visit, Xi's first trip to Washington since 2015, opening a summit expected to center on Taiwan, trade and artificial intelligence. Trump is scheduled to host Xi at the White House Thursday for a Rose Garden ceremony and state dinner, with both sides also expected to discuss establishing a bilateral AI dialogue mechanism.
