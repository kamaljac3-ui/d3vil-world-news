Americas

Venezuela frees 39 political prisoners after second round of US-backed talks

Venezuela released 39 political prisoners between Friday and Saturday, rights group Justicia, Encuentro y Perdón said, following a second round of talks between interim President Delcy Rodríguez's government and the opposition held with US approval. The releases included eight women held since 2021 on "narco-terrorism" charges and six people accused of aiding a 2020 attempt to remove former President Nicolás Maduro; rights group Foro Penal said 344 political prisoners remained jailed as of September 18, down from 391 in mid-August.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Manila Times (AP)

Europe

UK police evacuate homes, arrest several men near RAF base used in Iran war

Gloucestershire Police evacuated residents of Whelford, near RAF Fairford in western England, and arrested several men on suspicion of offenses under the Explosives Act, with army bomb-disposal specialists examining multiple vehicles. Fairford has hosted US bombers during strikes on Iran, but police said Sunday they had not confirmed any link between the arrests and the base, and that the situation was "contained."

Sources: WSLS (AP), NPR

Russian and Ukrainian strikes kill at least 10 as both sides trade aerial attacks

Russian and Ukrainian strikes killed at least 10 people combined on Saturday, officials on both sides said, including two women who died under the rubble of their home in Zaporizhzhia and two people killed in a separate Russian attack on Sumy. President Volodymyr Zelensky said Russia "is escalating every single day," as the exchanges continued even as Washington pushes for a new, low-level round of ceasefire talks.

Sources: Al Jazeera, ABC News (AP)

Middle East

France says it will send troops, air defenses to protect Saudi Arabia's Yanbu oil terminal

French President Emmanuel Macron said France will send soldiers, radar systems and air-defense equipment to help protect Saudi Arabia's Yanbu oil terminal on the Red Sea, though he gave no troop numbers, equipment details or arrival date. The announcement follows repeated Houthi missile and drone attacks on Yanbu, which has taken on added importance as an alternative export route since Iran's naval blockade disrupted Saudi shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Deployment timeline still undisclosed.

Sources: Arab News, Yahoo News (Reuters)

Saudi-led coalition intercepts Houthi missiles and drones, strikes Taiz in response

The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted two ballistic missiles and two drones launched by Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis toward Khamis Mushait and Riyadh over the weekend, and Saudi forces struck the Houthi-held city of Taiz in response. The exchange is the latest in an escalating campaign that has included Houthi strikes on Saudi energy infrastructure since the group declared a naval blockade against Riyadh in July.

Sources: US News (AP), Euronews

Africa

Separate mass shootings kill at least 27 in South African townships

Gunmen killed at least 27 people and wounded more than 20 in two separate shootings over the weekend, South African police said: eight assailants armed with AK-47 rifles and pistols killed at least 17 people at a tavern in Wedela, southwest of Johannesburg, late Saturday, and gunmen killed at least 10 more at a store in Lwandle, near Cape Town, early Sunday. No arrests had been made in either attack, which came days after a separate shooting at a house party near Durban killed 11 people, including a pregnant woman.

Sources: Al Jazeera, ABC News (AP)

Asia

Indonesia ferry death toll rises to 64 as search for dozens missing continues

The death toll from the September 13 sinking of the ferry Virgo Transport 8 in the Java Sea rose to 64 after search teams recovered 11 more bodies over the weekend, Indonesia's search and rescue agency Basarnas said, with 71 of the 243 people aboard still missing two weeks on. The ferry, sailing from Surabaya to Banjarmasin in rough weather, capsized on September 13, and Indonesia's transport minister said a full investigation will begin once the search concludes.

Sources: Antara, The Business Standard

India evacuates more than 70,000 as flash floods hit 13 Odisha districts

Odisha's state government evacuated more than 70,000 people from low-lying areas and opened 395 relief camps after swollen rivers and flash floods hit 13 districts following days of heavy rainfall, officials said Saturday. Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi was due to conduct an aerial survey of the worst-hit southern districts as authorities warned more rain was expected.

Sources: The Print (PTI), Siasat (PTI)
