Americas

Haiti indefinitely postpones long-delayed general election

Haiti's Provisional Electoral Council postponed the official campaign period and the first-round vote that had been scheduled for December 13, citing gang violence that has cut off main transport routes and left only about 769,000 of an estimated 11 million residents registered to vote. The council gave no new date and extended candidate registration to October 31; Haiti has not held an election since 2016 and has had no sitting president since Jovenel Moïse's 2021 assassination.

Sources: Yahoo News (Reuters), Jamaica Observer

Carney leaves Washington without a tariff deal after second Trump meeting

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney met President Trump at the White House on Tuesday for a second high-stakes round of trade talks, but left without an agreement on the sectoral tariffs hitting steel, aluminum, autos, energy and forestry. Trump said Canada would "love us again" and directed his cabinet to move quickly on sector deals, while Carney called the talks positive and said Canada would "get an even better deal," with both sides describing the relationship as still under negotiation.

Sources: CBC News, CTV News (The Canadian Press)

Europe

Russia launches one of its largest barrages of the war on Ukraine

Russia fired roughly 130 drones and dozens of cruise and ballistic missiles at Ukraine overnight into Wednesday, sending Kyiv residents into metro stations for shelter and striking the capital, Pryluky and Kremenchuk. Al Jazeera and France 24 reported at least eight to nine dead across the country citing Ukrainian officials, though other outlets cited higher regional tolls; a final nationwide count had not been agreed as of Wednesday.

Sources: Al Jazeera, France 24 (AFP)

Spain's Sánchez pushes through 21 housing decrees after Barcelona unrest

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez's government approved 21 "urgent" housing measures — including tighter short-term rental rules, extended eviction protections and a €10 billion loan fund for homebuyers — largely mirroring decrees Parliament had rejected the previous week. The package followed violent protests in Barcelona over Spain's housing crisis and comes as Sánchez heads into a snap election he called for November 29 after the original housing bills' defeat.

Sources: Yahoo News (AP), Euronews

Middle East

Saudi defenses intercept Houthi missile near Riyadh as Yemen offensive grinds on

The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi-launched ballistic missile north of Riyadh on Wednesday, days after the Iran-aligned group claimed an attack on the capital's main airport. The interception came as Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces press a major offensive to retake Houthi-held territory, a campaign both sides describe as intensifying since late September.

Sources: The Hill (AP), Al Arabiya English

Israeli strike kills two in Gaza as war marks third anniversary of Oct. 7

An Israeli strike on Gaza City overnight killed two people, including a civil defense volunteer, as Israel marked the third anniversary of Hamas's October 7, 2023 attack with memorial ceremonies at the Nova festival site and elsewhere. The ceasefire reached a year ago remains only partially implemented, with disputes continuing over the sequencing of Hamas's disarmament and further Israeli withdrawal.

Sources: Arab News, Vanguard (AFP)

Africa

Mali's army retakes Kidal from Tuareg rebels with Russian military help

Mali's army, backed by Russian Africa Corps air power, retook the northern town of Kidal after Tuareg-led separatists of the Azawad Liberation Front withdrew, saying they could not match Russia's use of air strikes. The town has changed hands repeatedly over the past two years; the junta is calling the recapture a symbolic win, though analysts say it is unlikely to be decisive against the wider jihadist and separatist insurgency in northern Mali.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Africanews

Kenya confirms first Ebola case and death, imported from Congo outbreak

Kenya's health ministry confirmed the country's first case of Bundibugyo ebolavirus after a Kenyan man who had lived in Congo for seven years died in Nairobi; he had traveled overland through Uganda before flying into the capital on October 3, and 28 contacts have since been identified and quarantined. Kenya is the fourth country to record a case tied to the eastern Congo outbreak, which government data there puts at more than 8,400 cases and over 4,000 deaths, the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak on record.

Sources: ABC News (AP), CTV News

Asia

Delhi police detain Rahul Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi and dozens of MPs in opposition march

Delhi police detained India's Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi, Congress MP Priyanka Gandhi Vadra and roughly 250 others, including some 50 lawmakers, after INDIA bloc opposition parties marched toward the Election Commission demanding the chief election commissioner's resignation over disputed voter-roll revisions. Gandhi and other leaders staged an eight-hour sit-in after being blocked near the commission's offices before being released Tuesday night, as police filed a complaint over their entry onto the grounds of the adjacent state broadcaster's building.

Sources: Business Standard (PTI), Deccan Herald

Japan lodges formal protest after US Marine arrested over Okinawa killing

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi protested to Washington after a 20-year-old US Marine stationed at Camp Futenma was arrested on suspicion of robbing and killing a 39-year-old woman in a Naha hotel; Japan's foreign and defense ministers separately summoned the US ambassador and the commander of US Forces Japan to lodge the protest. The US military imposed a curfew on its Okinawa personnel as the case renewed long-standing local anger over the roughly 18,000 Marines based on the island.

Sources: ABC News (AP), Al Jazeera
