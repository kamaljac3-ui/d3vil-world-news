Americas

Brazil's final presidential debate collapses as both frontrunners withdraw, bomb threat hits Supreme Court

Brazilian broadcaster TV Globo scrapped the last televised presidential debate on Thursday after President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and leading challenger Flávio Bolsonaro both pulled out, with Bolsonaro citing a last-minute court ruling he called "censorship" and Lula instead appearing on a podcast. A bomb threat separately forced an evacuation outside Brazil's Supreme Court the same day, leaving the country to head into Sunday's first-round vote without a single head-to-head debate between the two leading candidates.

Sources: Yahoo News (AP), MercoPress

Europe

Putin warns Russia would use "all weapons," including nuclear arms, if Kaliningrad is attacked

Speaking at Moscow's Valdai Forum on Thursday, Russian President Vladimir Putin said a direct attack on Kaliningrad, Russia's Baltic exclave, would make the use of the country's full arsenal "inevitably and unavoidably" come onto the agenda. The warning followed NATO military drills near Kaliningrad that Moscow has cast as a provocation; NATO has described the exercises as defensive, and Putin said he had no plans to attack any European country.

Sources: Yahoo News (AP), Al Jazeera

Russian drones strike a central Kyiv bridge three times in a day, halting Dnipro River crossing

Russian drones hit the support structure of Kyiv's Pivdennyi Bridge three times between Thursday and Friday, damaging a bridge pier, the roadway and a barrier along the subway line and forcing authorities to suspend metro and vehicle traffic across the Dnipro River crossing. City officials said no passengers were injured and that initial checks found no critical structural damage, though the repeated strikes reflect Russia's intensified use of jet-powered drones against Kyiv since late August.

Sources: Yahoo News (AP), Kyiv Independent

Middle East

US sends a third aircraft carrier and thousands of Marines toward the Gulf as Trump weighs new Iran strikes

The Pentagon is deploying the carrier USS Theodore Roosevelt and more than 10,000 additional sailors and Marines to the Middle East, officials said, a move that would put three carrier strike groups in the region for the first time since a fragile US-Iran ceasefire took hold in late February. President Trump has said renewed strikes on Iran are "possible" after November's midterm elections, and the buildup comes amid a wave of attacks on oil tankers transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

Sources: U.S. News & World Report (AP), Al Jazeera

Yemen's government strikes Houthi-held Sanaa after Saudi coalition accuses rebels of hitting power grid serving Medina

Yemen's internationally recognized government said its forces struck "high-value" Houthi targets in Sanaa and carried out roughly 20 further strikes in Taiz, Saada and Amran provinces on Friday, hours after the Saudi-led coalition accused the Houthis of a drone attack that knocked out a transformer at a power station supplying the Saudi city of Medina. It is the most serious flare-up between Saudi Arabia and the Iran-aligned Houthis since a UN-brokered truce largely halted cross-border attacks in 2022; the Houthis denied responsibility for the Medina strike.

Sources: Yahoo News (Reuters), Arab News

Africa

Ethiopia and Eritrea sever diplomatic ties as Tigray fighting threatens to draw in the neighbors

Ethiopia expelled 10 Eritrean diplomats and closed its embassy in Asmara on Thursday, accusing Eritrean personnel of posing "a direct threat" to national security, prompting Eritrea to announce it was severing all diplomatic relations in response. Addis Ababa has accused Eritrea of backing an alliance of armed groups fighting Ethiopian government forces across Tigray, Amhara and Afar — an accusation Asmara denies — reversing the 2018 peace agreement that had ended two decades of hostility between the neighbors.

Sources: Yahoo News (AP), Al Jazeera

Asia

Xi hardens National Day rhetoric on Taiwan "reunification" days after urging Trump to oppose independence

Chinese President Xi Jinping told a National Day reception in Beijing that China must "resolutely crack down" on Taiwanese independence activity and "advance the great cause of national reunification," using notably sharper language than in his speech a year earlier. The remarks came days after Xi's state visit to Washington, where he had pressed President Trump to explicitly oppose Taiwanese independence and limit US support for the self-governing island.

Sources: Reuters (via Internazionale), Taipei Times

Thailand's worst flooding in 15 years kills at least 23 as government approves emergency aid

Flooding that has battered Thailand since mid-September has killed at least 23 people and affected more than 3 million residents across 29 provinces and Bangkok, authorities said, in what officials called the capital's worst flooding in 15 years. Thailand's government approved roughly $122 million in emergency relief funds for flood victims as floodwaters began receding in Bangkok this week, though authorities said full drainage could take up to another week.

Sources: ABC News (AP), Bangkok Post
