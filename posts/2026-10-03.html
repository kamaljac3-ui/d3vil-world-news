Americas

Brazil heads into Sunday's vote with Lula seeking a historic fourth term against Flávio Bolsonaro

Brazilians vote Sunday in a first round that could hand President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, 80, an unprecedented fourth term, with polls showing him narrowly ahead of Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, who is barred from running after being convicted over a coup plot. Recent BTG Pactual/Nexus polling put Lula's first-round support around 42% to Bolsonaro's 37%, with the two locked in a tighter statistical tie for a potential October 25 runoff.

Pakistan locks down Islamabad, imposes emergency public-gathering bans ahead of Imran Khan party's planned Friday march

Punjab authorities imposed public-gathering bans in Faisalabad, Bahawalpur and Mianwali and Islamabad blocked major highways with shipping containers this week, as Imran Khan's Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf mobilizes for a long march to the capital it has set for Friday, October 4, to demand the jailed former prime minister's release. The march, originally planned for late September, was postponed amid a heavy security crackdown that party officials say has already seen clashes with police and mass arrests of supporters in preceding regional rallies.
