Americas

Indefinite truckers' strike blocks highways across Chile over fuel prices

Small and mid-sized trucking unions began an indefinite nationwide strike on November 10, partially blocking stretches of Route 5 and other highways after talks over sustained fuel-price increases broke down, according to EFE. President José Antonio Kast's government has offered a bill capping future fuel-price increases for 90 days, but the Fuerza del Norte confederation rejected the terms and continued negotiating in Santiago this week while blockades persisted in the south and north. It is the first trucker strike of Kast's presidency, and producers warn it threatens fresh-produce exports just as the harvest season begins.

Sources: EFE (via Infobae), Rio Times

Two Ecuadorian local candidates shot dead weeks before election, OAS condemns killing

Gunmen opened fire on a political gathering in Huaquillas on the night of November 9, killing mayoral candidate Ronald Farfán and council candidate Cristhian Rugel along with a bystander, according to EFE. Police are investigating possible links to extortion by organized-crime groups in El Oro province, which has recorded nearly 1,000 homicides this year, about 50% more than the same period last year. The OAS's electoral mission in Ecuador condemned the killings and called for the country's November 29 local elections to proceed in "a climate of peace."

Sources: EFE (via Infobae), Rio Times

Europe

Spain holds its first general strike in 14 years over housing and cost of living

Spain's two largest unions, UGT and CCOO, led a general strike on Wednesday that unions said drew about four million participants, the country's first such strike since 2012, according to Reuters. The walkout, backed by tenants' groups protesting rents and evictions, came ahead of a snap general election Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez has called for November 29 after losing a parliamentary vote on housing decrees. Authorities reported dozens of arrests tied to road blockades and clashes with police during the first hours of the strike.

Sources: Reuters (via CNN), UPI

Slovakia's Fico accuses Germany's Merz of "blackmail" in EU budget standoff over Ukraine aid

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz told European Council President António Costa in a letter that all EU members must commit to covering a projected €70 billion Ukraine funding gap for 2027 before the bloc's next long-term budget talks proceed, according to Reuters. Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico called the demand "blackmail," noting he holds the same veto power, and the Czech Republic's Andrej Babiš backed him, while Hungary's Péter Magyar said the two issues should not be linked. EU leaders are expected to take up Ukraine financing at an informal summit in Dublin on November 13 ahead of a dedicated budget summit later this month.

Sources: Reuters (via Internazionale), Euronews

Middle East

Israeli strike on Gaza's Nasser hospital kills six, including a surgeon

An Israeli airstrike hit Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis overnight, killing six people, including a general surgeon, and wounding at least a dozen more, according to the AP, citing Gaza's Health Ministry. The Israeli military said it had struck a Hamas operative using the hospital complex, without providing evidence, while medical staff said the strike hit the surgical wing just after midnight. The strike adds to a string of deadly incidents that have continued despite the ceasefire reached in October 2025, which negotiators have yet to translate into a full Israeli withdrawal or Hamas disarmament.

Sources: AP (via NPR), ABC News Australia

Saudi-led coalition intercepts Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Mecca region

The Saudi-led military coalition said it destroyed three cruise missiles headed toward Riyadh and two drones aimed at the Mecca region and southern Saudi Arabia on Tuesday, according to Reuters. Houthi spokesperson Yahya Saree denied the group targeted Mecca, saying its operations strike only oil facilities and military sites, a claim Saudi officials have not addressed. The interception follows a Houthi attack that damaged an Aramco administrative building in Riyadh days earlier and an October strike on Riyadh's main airport that killed 13 people.

Sources: Reuters (via Yahoo News), Middle East Eye

Africa

Congo's Ebola outbreak tops 11,000 cases, now the second-deadliest on record

Congo's National Institute of Public Health said Wednesday that the outbreak has reached 11,042 confirmed cases and 5,387 deaths, surpassing 11,000 cases for the first time, according to the AP. The Bundibugyo-strain outbreak, which has no approved vaccine or treatment, has spread to eight of the country's 26 provinces and is now the second-largest and second-deadliest Ebola epidemic on record, behind only the 2014-2016 West African outbreak, Al Jazeera reported. Health authorities are appealing for a halt to fighting in Ituri and North Kivu provinces so workers can reach the sick.

Sources: AP (via ClickOrlando), Al Jazeera

Kenya discharges its last Ebola patient, starts 42-day countdown to disease-free status

Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient on Wednesday, Health Minister Aden Duale said, starting a 42-day countdown toward a declaration that the country is free of the disease, according to Reuters. Of the nine confirmed cases since the outbreak began in early October, seven patients fully recovered while the first patient and his mother died, the AP reported. The World Health Organization's Africa office said Kenya could be declared Ebola-free on December 23 if no new case is recorded in the meantime.

Sources: AP (via NBC News), WHO Africa

Asia

Indonesia's vice president formally resigns as lawmakers scrap their impeachment motion

Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka submitted his resignation to President Prabowo Subianto and parliament on Thursday, days after court rulings invalidated the high-school equivalency certificate he used to qualify for the 2024 ballot, according to Al Jazeera. The move came after 120 lawmakers from all eight parliamentary parties filed a motion on Wednesday that would have been the first formal step toward impeaching him, a motion the House of Representatives then dropped once he resigned. Indonesia's constitution does not require a new election to fill the vacancy, and it remains unclear who Prabowo will nominate to replace him.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Reuters (via South China Morning Post)

G7 foreign ministers warn China's activity east of Taiwan threatens regional stability

G7 foreign ministers issued a joint declaration after meeting in Paris warning that China's stepped-up coast guard and military activity in waters east of Taiwan threatens regional stability and freedom of navigation, according to Reuters. Taiwanese officials believe Beijing is rehearsing for a potential blockade using overlapping military, paramilitary and civilian assets, and Reuters described the statement as the strongest unified G7 expression of concern on the issue to date. Taiwan's presidential office welcomed the declaration, while Chinese leader Xi Jinping separately said in a speech that Taiwan's "reunification" with the mainland "must happen" and pledged to pursue it peacefully.

Sources: Reuters (via Taipei Times), Reuters (via WHBL)
